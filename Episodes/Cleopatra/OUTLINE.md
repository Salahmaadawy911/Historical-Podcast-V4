## Arc — the central question, and what each part answers

**Arc:** *Did she seduce Rome — or hold it off for twenty years with the only weapons Egypt had left? And
who gets to write the answer?* Part 1 puts the charge (statecraft or surrender) and ends at the moment
it is sharpest; Part 2 answers it and closes on her own last word.
