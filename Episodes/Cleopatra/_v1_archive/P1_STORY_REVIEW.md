## Where a viewer is most likely to leave
**0:44–3:19.** The cold open promises a woman answering two thousand years of *"seductress"*; Act A then spends two and a half minutes on inheritance, debt and administration before the first vivid thing (Pompey's head, 3:35). Every line is good; the *order* front-loads the slowest material into the steepest part of the retention curve.

## Cold-reader pass — what a newcomer would not follow
1. **Actium (P1_082):** *"The fleets meet"* — whose fleets? The card says where and when, not who fought whom. → **Fix the card, no generation:** *"Headland off western Greece. On 2 September 31 BC Octavian's fleet met Antony's and Cleopatra's there."*
2. **P1_019:** *"a dead cousin of his was supposed to have willed our kingdom to Rome"* — which cousin, which will? Hedged correctly, but opaque. Acceptable as her aside; a context card would over-explain it.
3. **P1_015:** *"recognition as a friend of Rome"* is a formal title, and reads as a figure of speech. Acceptable.
